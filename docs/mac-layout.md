# Mac layout: a sidebar instead of the pager

Agreed on 19 October 2026 from the prototypes on `proto-wide-layout` (`--demo --prototype sidebar --section today|pots|month|entries|plan`).

## The window

- A sidebar and one pane, about 920 × 620; resizable, with a minimum size, and it keeps the size it was left at.
- Same black glass. The light along the bottom edge takes the colours of the section showing.

## The sidebar (about 210 wide)

- Today (with how many things are still to pay), Pots, Month.
- Under "Records": Entries (with this month's count), Plan.
- At the bottom: "You can spend" with the amount a day (hidden while Today shows, as it says it already), and
  **Log a Spend** (⌘N).
- ⌘1 to ⌘5 go to the five sections.

## The sections

- **Today:** the date as "Monday 19 October"; the ring on the left; on the right Still to pay and "Spent today",
  in a column of capped width so amounts sit near their names.
- **Pots:** as now, tubes shorter so they don't run the pane's full height.
- **Month:** as now; the "Entries" row goes (Entries is in the sidebar).
- **Entries:** the month's list in the pane, with the month to choose.
- **Plan:** the plan's list in the pane, with Add Item.

## Editors and sheets

- An entry or a plan item opens in a **panel on the right**, beside the list (an inspector), not over it.
- **Quick log** rises as a **drawer over the pane**, as now. Payday and Set Balance stay drawers too.
  (Changed on 19 October: they are now cards in the middle of the window, over the rest of it blurred.)

## Goes away

The paging ScrollView, the page dots, arrow keys between screens, the Plan and + buttons at the bottom, the Plan and
Entries drawers.

## Not in this round

A menu bar item for Quick log; categories of your own.
